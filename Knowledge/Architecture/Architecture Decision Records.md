# Architecture decision records

## Capture the decision

Record context, the proposed decision, alternatives, consequences, and the evidence that would cause reconsideration. Keep one decision per note and use the Decision template. An ADR's decision can be proposed while the note's review status remains draft.

## Maintain the record

Link requirements and affected interfaces. When changing direction, write a new decision and link the superseded record rather than erasing its rationale. See the [ADR community](https://adr.github.io/) for established approaches.

The synthetic [[Projects/Example Issue Tracker/Decision 001 Modular Monolith]] is a proposal, not a production architecture claim.
